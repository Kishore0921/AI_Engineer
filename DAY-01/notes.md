# Day 1 — Introduction to AI Engineering

## Learning objective
Understand what AI engineering is, how the role differs from machine learning engineering, and where AI fits in products and the broader ecosystem.

## Topics and subtopics
1. What is an AI Engineer?
2. Roles and responsibilities of an AI Engineer.
3. AI Engineer vs ML Engineer.
4. AI vs AGI.
5. Impact of AI on product development.
6. AI ecosystem.

## Important concepts
- AI engineering applies models and software engineering to build useful AI-powered products.
- AI solutions involve more than a model: data, interfaces, evaluation, deployment, and user needs matter.
- AI usually refers to systems performing specific tasks; AGI is a hypothetical broader, general capability.
- AI and ML engineering roles overlap, but AI engineers often focus on integrating models into applications.

## Key terminology
AI engineer, ML engineer, artificial intelligence, artificial general intelligence (AGI), model, AI ecosystem, product development.

## Learning outcomes
- Describe typical AI engineering responsibilities and distinguish them from ML engineering.
- Explain the difference between task-focused AI and the AGI concept.
- Map the main people, models, platforms, and product components in an AI ecosystem.

# Day 9 — AI Safety and Security

## Learning objective
Identify common risks in AI applications, including prompt injection, privacy concerns, bias, and fairness.

## Topics and subtopics
1. Prompt injection attacks.
2. Security and privacy.
3. Bias in AI systems.
4. Fairness.
5. AI safety.
6. Potential risks of AI applications.

## Important concepts
- Prompt injection attempts to influence model behavior through untrusted instructions in inputs or retrieved content.
- Treat user-provided and retrieved text as untrusted; model instructions alone are not a security boundary.
- Minimize sensitive data and consider where prompts, outputs, and logs are processed or retained.
- Bias and unfair outcomes may arise from data, system design, or usage; evaluate across relevant groups.
- Record risks, impact, likelihood, and practical mitigations instead of assuming a model is safe.

## Key terminology
Prompt injection, untrusted input, security, privacy, bias, fairness, AI safety, risk register, mitigation.

## Learning outcomes
- Explain prompt injection and identify potentially sensitive data paths.
- Distinguish bias and fairness concerns from ordinary output quality.
- Create test cases and a practical AI risk register.

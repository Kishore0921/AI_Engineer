# Day 28 — Building Multimodal AI Applications

## Learning objective
Combine modalities in an application workflow and compare framework concepts for multimodal integration.

## Topics and subtopics
1. LangChain multimodal applications.
2. LlamaIndex multimodal applications.
3. Multimodal integration.
4. Combining different modalities.
5. Multimodal application workflows.

## Important concepts
- A multimodal workflow coordinates inputs and outputs from two or more modalities, such as text, image, and audio.
- Framework support and model compatibility vary; verify that components can exchange the required data.
- Keep modality-specific preprocessing and error states explicit.
- Test the end-to-end workflow with safe sample inputs and provide useful fallback behavior when a modality fails.

## Key terminology
Multimodal integration, modality, LangChain, LlamaIndex, preprocessing, workflow, cross-modal input/output.

## Learning outcomes
- Explain where LangChain or LlamaIndex may fit into a multimodal workflow.
- Connect at least two modalities in a small prototype.
- Document component compatibility, workflow steps, and failure cases.

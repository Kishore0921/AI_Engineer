## Important concepts
- Cloud platforms host model access or machine-learning workflows with provider-specific configuration, pricing, and data terms.
- Hosted inference trades local compute requirements for network, provider, and service dependencies.
- Local inference can improve control over data flow but brings hardware and maintenance needs.

# Day 27 — Audio and Video AI

## Learning objective
Understand speech/audio workflows, the Whisper API, text-to-speech, and introductory video understanding.

## Topics and subtopics
1. Audio processing.
2. Whisper API.
3. Speech-to-text.
4. Text-to-speech.
5. Video understanding.
6. Audio AI applications.

## Important concepts
- Speech-to-text converts audio into text; transcription can contain recognition errors.
- Text-to-speech generates spoken audio from text and may have voice, latency, and usage constraints.
- Whisper API is one transcription option; check current supported models and input limits.
- Video understanding may rely on extracting or sampling visual/audio content; results depend on coverage and context.
- Obtain consent for recordings and avoid uploading sensitive audio without authorization.

## Key terminology
Audio processing, transcription, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, Whisper API, video understanding, voice AI.

## Learning outcomes
- Describe core audio and video AI workflows.
- Build a voice AI prototype using an available transcription/speech capability.
- Evaluate transcription and generated-speech limitations and data considerations.

# Day 27 Practical — Build a voice AI prototype

## Practical objective
Create a small voice workflow that processes speech and returns a useful text or spoken response.

## Exact practical output
**Build a voice AI prototype.**

## Tasks
1. Plan a short voice interaction and obtain/record permission for any audio used.
2. Use a documented Whisper API or another available speech-to-text route to transcribe a short test clip.
3. Review the transcript for recognition errors and record the original audio's source/consent.
4. Optionally connect the transcript to a simple response and use a documented text-to-speech option to speak it.
5. Test a clear and a more difficult sample, then note transcription, pronunciation, privacy, and service limitations.

## Expected result
A voice AI prototype demonstrating speech-to-text and, where available, a spoken response, with consent and error notes.

## Completion checklist
- [ ] Audio input is permitted and non-sensitive.
- [ ] Speech-to-text is demonstrated or access blocker is recorded.
- [ ] Transcript is checked against the audio.
- [ ] Text-to-speech is tested where available.
- [ ] Privacy and recognition limitations are documented.

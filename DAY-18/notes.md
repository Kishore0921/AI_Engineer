## Important concepts
- Vector databases store vectors and associated metadata and support similarity-oriented retrieval.
- Functionality can include indexing, filtering, persistence, updates, and search, but differs by product and deployment.
- Some tools are local libraries while others are managed services or broader databases with vector features.
- Choose based on data scale, operational needs, filtering, persistence, pricing, privacy, and integration.

## Key terminology
Vector database, vector index, metadata, similarity search, filtering, persistence, managed service, local library.
